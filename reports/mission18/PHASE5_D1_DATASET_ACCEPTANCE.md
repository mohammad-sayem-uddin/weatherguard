## 2. Non-Negotiable Dataset Acceptance Protocol

The moment the raw synthetic corpus arrives, the following automated gates must be evaluated before the dataset can be accepted for experimental training:

### 2.1. Structural Integrity Gates
1. **Resolution Conformity:** Exactly $1280 \times 720$ native resolution. Zero silent cropping or aspect ratio distortion.
2. **Image Reference Completeness:** 100% of annotation records must correspond to readable image files on disk; zero orphaned annotations or dangling image paths.
3. **Exact Duplicates:** Byte-level MD5 / SHA-256 collisions must be strictly rejected ($0\text{ tolerance}$).
4. **Perceptual Near-Duplicates:** Images with dHash Hamming distance $\le 4$ must be flagged and clustered into atomic components.

### 2.2. Semantic & Annotation Quality Gates
1. **Canonical Class Taxonomy:** Every valid box must belong strictly to one of the 6 canonical classes:
   - `0: truck`, `1: person`, `2: bicycle`, `3: car`, `4: motorcycle`, `5: bus`.
   - Zero tolerance for out-of-vocabulary or unmapped classes.
2. **Axis & Coordinate Integrity:** Coordinates must strictly adhere to the Gemini native adapter $[y_{\min}, x_{\min}, y_{\max}, x_{\max}]$ normalized to $[0..1000]$ or canonical dictionary space, with zero X/Y or width/height swapping.
3. **Boundary Adherence:** All boxes must reside in $[0, 1000]$ normalized space. Boxes with width or height $< 2\text{px}$ or area $< 4\text{px}^2$ must be rejected.
4. **Duplicate Pruning:** Intra-image bounding boxes sharing the same class with $\text{IoU} \ge 0.85$ must be pruned.

### 2.3. Partitioning & Leakage-Safety Gates
1. **Global DSU Clustering:** All scenario families and transitive dHash chains must be clustered globally across all weather conditions.
2. **Zero Overlap:**
   $$S_{\text{train}} \cap S_{\text{val}} = \emptyset, \quad S_{\text{train}} \cap S_{\text{test}} = \emptyset, \quad S_{\text{val}} \cap S_{\text{test}} = \emptyset$$
3. **Internal Test Lock:** The 400 internal test images (20%) must be cryptographically locked (`artifacts/d1_splits_manifest.json`) and held completely frozen. Zero threshold tuning or architecture selection on test images.
