## 3. Historical Metric and Protocol Inconsistencies

During the audit of historical evaluation artifacts from Missions 1–15, the following critical methodological boundaries were cataloged:

1. **Still Image vs Temporal Video Discrepancy:**
   - Missions 9, 10, 14, and 15 evaluated video sequences from BDD100K (e.g., Policy J and Policy K leveraging optical flow, temporal tracking, and causal contrast accumulation).
   - WeatherGuard-D1 is composed of **independent still synthetic frames**.
   - **Protocol Safeguard:** Applying Policy K to still images must be clearly understood as operating without temporal accumulation ($T=1$). Still images cannot be used to evaluate or claim temporal flicker reduction or multi-frame tracking continuity.

2. **Metric Definition Evolution:**
   - Missions 1–13 primarily reported single-threshold $F_1$ scores at fixed confidence (0.20) and IoU (0.50).
   - Mission 17 introduced continuous 101-point interpolated mAP@0.50 and mAP@0.50:0.95.
   - **Protocol Safeguard:** In all Mission 18 baseline comparisons, both single-threshold $F_1$ (for exact historical comparability) and 101-point mAP (for standard modern computer vision benchmarking) will be reported in parallel.

3. **Class Taxonomy Differences:**
   - DAWN: 6 classes (`bicycle`, `bus`, `car`, `motorcycle`, `person`, `truck`).
   - BDD100K: 10 classes mapped to 6 canonical classes.
   - FLIR ADAS: Thermal single-band IR, primarily cars and pedestrians (bicycles/motorcycles rare, trucks/buses variable).
   - WeatherGuard-D1: Custom 6 classes ordered `[truck, person, bicycle, car, motorcycle, bus]`.
   - **Protocol Safeguard:** All cross-domain evaluations must explicitly invoke `resolve_model_class_mapping()` and document unrepresented classes.
