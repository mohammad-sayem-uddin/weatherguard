## 1. Executive Summary

Mission 22 was conducted as a rigorous, independent forensic audit and scientific qualification of the multi-modal search-and-rescue (SAR) architecture developed across Missions 20 and 21. While Mission 21 reported impressive preliminary gains—including a $+36.5\text{ pp}$ boost in $F_1$ over vision alone, a $3.8\times$ battery endurance extension, and confirmed generalization across 33 novel human speakers—Mission 22 audited the underlying codebase, data pipelines, scoring assumptions, and physical aerodynamic models to separate genuine algorithmic breakthroughs from simulation artifacts.

### Key Forensic Audit & Empirical Findings:
1. **Asynchronous Constructed Pairings (Not Physical Simultaneity):**  
   The 450 reported multi-modal trials were **not** 450 simultaneous video-audio recordings from physical drone flights. They were synthetic scenario pairings generated by crossing 160 frozen visual frames from WeatherGuard with audio recordings from Rescue Drone V2.
2. **The "Phantom Person" Ground-Truth Defect:**  
   In 143 out of 315 positive trials (**45.4%**), ground truth was forced to `True` because an audio distress clip was attached, even though the camera image contained **zero visible persons**. Because the image was devoid of victims, the Vision-Only detector predictably reported no detection. This was penalized as a False Negative (miss) against Vision-Only. In fact, **143 out of the 208 reported Vision-Only misses (68.75%)** were phantom-person artifacts where no target existed to be seen. Correcting for this scoring bias, the true vision miss rate on frames actually containing a victim was **$37.79\%$**, not the reported $66.03\%$.
3. **Speaker Independence Disproven on Emergency Distress Cries:**  
   Mission 21 claimed 33 novel, unseen human speakers in the test split. Our provenance trace revealed that **exactly 33 of these 34 unseen speakers were negative-class background talkers** from Mozilla Common Voice. Only **one individual** was a novel human distress speaker. When evaluated on truly unseen speakers ($N=62$), model $F_1$ dropped from $0.9267$ to **$0.7143$**, and the missed distress call rate surged nearly four-fold from $7.33\%$ to **$28.57\%$** (2 out of 7 novel human distress cries were missed).
4. **Adaptive Fusion vs. Simple Heuristic OR:**  
   McNemar's exact paired test between Adaptive Fusion and Simple OR yielded $p = 0.1797$ ($p > 0.05$). **Adaptive Fusion did not achieve a statistically significant improvement in $F_1$ over Simple OR** (Simple OR attained $F_1 = 0.8790$ vs. Adaptive Fusion $F_1 = 0.8685$). Rather, the true operational value of Adaptive Fusion is a **$14.3\%$ relative reduction in false deployments** (FAR reduced from $10.37\%$ to $8.89\%$, avoiding false drone dispatch).
5. **Propulsion Aerodynamics vs. Compute Power:**  
   The claimed $3.8\times$ battery endurance multiplier applies **strictly to companion computer payload power** ($18.5\text{ W} \to 4.84\text{ W}$). For a standard $1.5\text{ kg}$ quadrotor, motor hover propulsion consumes $\approx 180-220\text{ W}$, completely dwarfing compute drain. On a shared flight battery ($59.2\text{ Wh}$), duty-cycling extends total flight time by **$+6.86\%$ (+1.2 minutes: $17.5\text{ min} \to 18.7\text{ min}$)**. Representing duty-cycling as extending drone flight from 18 to 68 minutes was aerodynamically invalid.
6. **Scientific Qualification Matrix Established:**  
   Every claim from Missions 20–22 has been formally qualified with one of seven strict scientific statuses: `VERIFIED`, `REPRODUCED`, `PRELIMINARY`, `SIMULATION_ONLY`, `ENGINEERING_ESTIMATE`, `NOT_VERIFIED`, or `BLOCKED_PENDING_DATA`.

## 4. Corrected Metrics & The Phantom-Person Defect

### 4.1 Root Cause of the Vision-Only Under-Reporting
In Mission 21 (`run_multimodal_controlled_experiments.py`), composite ground truth $y_{\text{composite}}$ was constructed using an alternating assignment logic:
- Positive audio sample attached $\implies y_{\text{composite}} = \text{True}$.
- Negative audio sample attached $\implies y_{\text{composite}} = y_{\text{vision}}$.

This generated **three disjoint classes of positive trials**:
1. **Bimodal Positive ($N=86$, $27.3\%$):** Person visually visible in camera frame AND emergency distress cry present in audio clip.
2. **Phantom-Person Positive ($N=143$, $45.4\%$):** Distress cry present in audio clip, but the paired camera frame contained **ZERO visible persons**.
3. **Silent-Person Positive ($N=86$, $27.3\%$):** Person visible in camera frame, but audio clip was ordinary negative conversation.

```
       [ 450 Multi-Modal Controlled Simulation Trials ]
                              |
              +---------------+---------------+
              |                               |
       [ 135 Negatives ]               [ 315 Positives ]
       (No Person, No Cry)                    |
                                +-------------+-------------+
                                |             |             |
                             [ 86 ]        [ 143 ]        [ 86 ]
                            Bimodal       Phantom-       Silent-
                            Positive       Person        Person
                            (Vis & Aud)  (Audio Only)  (Vision Only)
```

### 4.2 Scoring Distortion
Because the camera image in phantom-person trials was completely empty of humans, YOLOv8n predictably output zero detections. Under the composite ground-truth label ($y = \text{True}$), this was tallied as a **False Negative (miss)** against Vision-Only:
- Reported Vision-Only Misses: **$208$**
- Phantom Misses (No person in frame): **$143$ ($68.75\%$ of all reported vision misses!)**
- Real Vision Misses (Person visible in frame): **$65$**

### 4.3 Corrected Comparison Ledger

| Metric | Mission 21 Reported (Flawed Composite GT) | Mission 22 Corrected (Optical Frames Containing Persons) | Methodological Correction |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Vision-Only Recall** | $33.97\%$ (107 / 315) | **$62.21\%$** (107 / 172) | Excludes 143 phantom frames devoid of human victims |
| **Vision-Only Miss Rate** | $66.03\%$ (208 / 315) | **$37.79\%$** (65 / 172) | True failure rate when targets are physically visible |
| **Vision Precision** | $97.27\%$ (107 / 110) | **$97.27\%$** (107 / 110) | Unchanged (3 false positive detections) |
| **Vision False Alarm Rate** | $2.22\%$ (3 / 135) | **$2.22\%$** (3 / 135) | Unchanged |
| **Audio-Only Miss Rate** | $31.75\%$ (100 / 315) | **$6.12\%$** (14 / 229) | Evaluated on audio clips containing actual distress cries |
| **Audio False Alarm Rate** | $8.15\%$ (11 / 135) | **$7.33\%$** (11 / 150) | Evaluated against all negative audio controls |

**Conclusion:** The claim that vision alone misses $66\%$ of victims in search-and-rescue was inflated by penalizing the vision model for failing to detect sound waves in empty images. On frames where persons are visually present, the fine-tuned vision model achieves $62.2\%$ recall. The true value of multi-modal fusion is **recovering the $37.8\%$ of visually obscured victims and alerting to distant victims outside the camera's optical horizon**.

## 7. Fusion Calibration & Safety Dynamics

### 7.1 Leak-Free Platt Scaling (Validation Split Only)
In Mission 20, the operational threshold $\tau^* = 0.54$ was derived by sweeping directly on the locked test split. In Mission 22, logistic calibration was implemented strictly on the **Validation split** ($N=300$, 92 parent recordings):
$$P(\text{distress} \mid z) = \frac{1}{1 + \exp(-(a \cdot z + b))}, \quad a = 0.9245, \ b = -0.6641$$

- **Expected Calibration Error (ECE):** Reduced from $0.0369 \to \mathbf{0.0276}$ (**$-25.2\%$ relative reduction**).
- **Brier Score:** Improved from $0.0562 \to \mathbf{0.0536}$.
- **Validation Optimal Decision Threshold $(\tau_{\text{val}}^*)$:** Derived as **$0.38$** (zero test-set leakage).

### 7.2 Safety-Critical Operational States
The upgraded `FusionEngine` (`src/multimodal/fusion_engine.py`) implements a 7-state safety taxonomy:
1. `ACOUSTIC_DISTRESS_DETECTED`: Validated acoustic distress; camera dispatches to search; **zero visual coordinates asserted**.
2. `VISUAL_PERSON_DETECTED`: Person visually detected in camera frame; victim is silent or occluded.
3. `PERSON_VISUALLY_CORROBORATED`: Concordant agreement between optical and acoustic sensors.
4. `SEARCH_REQUIRED`: Ambiguous acoustic cue triggers optical spiral search.
5. `UNCERTAIN_EVIDENCE`: Low-margin sensor conflict triggers explicit abstention.
6. `SENSOR_UNAVAILABLE`: Sensor hardware fault flagged.
7. `NO_TARGET`: Clean negative across both modalities.

### 7.3 Continuous Flight False Alarm Dynamics
At a 10 Hz acoustic sampling rate (36,000 evaluations/hour):
- An unconstrained $7.33\%$ false positive rate produces **2,638.8 false alarms/hour**, creating unusable operational chatter.
- A temporal 2-consecutive alert gating filter ($0.2\text{ s}$ latency window) suppresses uncorrelated false alarms by $92.7\%$ relative:
  $$\text{Gated FPR} \approx \text{FPR}^2 = (0.0733)^2 = 0.00537 \implies \mathbf{193.4\text{ alerts/hour}}$$

## 8. Synchronized Data Collection Protocol

To replace synthetic scenario pairings with physical ground truth, Phase 5 formulated `configs/multimodal_synchronized_schema.yaml` and `PHASE5_SYNCHRONIZED_DATA_COLLECTION_PROTOCOL.md`.

### 8.1 Hardware Synchronization Architecture
- **Clock Master:** GPS-synchronized Pulse-Per-Second (PPS) or PTP (IEEE 1588) with microsecond precision.
- **Camera:** Global shutter RGB sensor ($1920\times 1080$ at $30\text{ FPS}$), logging exposure midpoint in nanoseconds (`frame_timestamp_utc_ns`).
- **Microphone:** Digital $I^2S$ MEMS omnidirectional transducer ($16\text{ kHz}$ 16-bit linear PCM), logging buffer midpoint (`audio_window_mid_ns`).
- **Permissible Drift Limit:**
  $$\Delta t = |t_{\text{frame\_mid}} - t_{\text{audio\_window\_mid}}| \le 5.0\text{ ms}$$
  Any record exceeding $5.0\text{ ms}$ drift is discarded as `SYNC_DRIFT_EXCEEDED`.

### 8.2 Four-Tier Dataset Hierarchy
1. `TIER_1_FIELD_SYNCHRONIZED`: Real UAV flight over staged human volunteers outdoors with simultaneous GPS clock sync.
2. `TIER_2_STAGED_SYNCHRONIZED`: Controlled ground-truth field recordings (static mount, synchronized clock).
3. `TIER_3_BENCHMARK_AUGMENTED`: Physical audio/video augmented with calibrated acoustic environmental noise.
4. `TIER_4_SYNTHETIC_SIMULATION`: Asynchronous pairings and synthetic voices (Missions 20–21).

### 8.3 Cross-Modal Identity Association Schema
Every bounding box and audio segment carries an explicit association link:
- `same_individual`: Visible person is confirmed as the vocalizing victim.
- `different_individual`: Visible person is a silent bystander; distress cry is from an unseen third party.
- `visual_person_silent`: Visible person is silent.
- `audio_person_occluded`: Distress cry heard, but victim is obscured behind vegetation/structures.

## 9. Edge Power & Flight Aerodynamics Reconciliation

### 9.1 Resolution of the $3.8\times$ Flight Extension Discrepancy
The $3.82\times$ power reduction was derived purely from compute payload duty-cycling:
$$P_{\text{avg}} = P_{\text{standby}} \cdot (1 - d) + P_{\text{active}} \cdot d = 2.8 \times 0.87 + 18.5 \times 0.13 = 4.84\text{ W}$$
$$\frac{P_{\text{active}}}{P_{\text{avg}}} = \frac{18.5\text{ W}}{4.84\text{ W}} = \mathbf{3.82\times}$$

### 9.2 Propulsion Physics Reality Check

| Subsystem Level | Continuous Tracking Mode | Duty-Cycled Mode ($d=0.13$) | Battery Specification | Measured Endurance | Endurance Gain |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Case A: Compute Payload Only** | $18.5\text{ W}$ | $4.84\text{ W}$ | Dedicated $2\text{S}$ LiPo ($5.92\text{ Wh}$) | $19.2\text{ min} \to 73.4\text{ min}$ | **$+282\%$ ($3.82\times$)** |
| **Case B: Full Multirotor Drone** | $203.5\text{ W}$ | $189.84\text{ W}$ | Shared $4\text{S}$ LiPo ($59.2\text{ Wh}$) | $17.5\text{ min} \to 18.7\text{ min}$ | **$+6.86\%$ (+1.2 min)** |

**Engineering Analysis:** For a $1.5\text{ kg}$ quadrotor, motor hover propulsion consumes $\approx 180\text{ W}$. Compute duty-cycling reduces total drone power by $13.66\text{ W}$ ($6.7\%$), providing an extra **$1.2\text{ minutes}$** of flight time. Future papers must distinguish compute payload endurance from physical drone endurance.

### 9.3 Hardware Profiling on Apple M1 CPU
- **Vision YOLOv8n ($640\times 640$ FP32):** Median latency = **$51.46\text{ ms}$** ($19.4\text{ FPS}$). Parameter count: $3,139,218$.
- **Audio Standard CNN ($64\times 126$ Spectrogram):** Median latency = **$1.39\text{ ms}$** ($719.4\text{ windows/s}$). Parameter count: $1,076,417$.
- **Audio Lightweight Separable CNN:** Median latency = **$0.98\text{ ms}$** ($1,020.4\text{ windows/s}$). Parameter count: $6,705$.
- **Raspberry Pi 4B/5 Targets:** Reclassified as **UNMEASURED ENGINEERING PROJECTIONS**.

## 10. Research Limitations

Honest recognition of limitations is essential to scientific credibility:
1. **Asynchronous Scenario Pairings:** Current benchmark trials do not represent simultaneous physical captures.
2. **Small Cluster Count ($K=4$):** All 450 visual trials originate from only 4 underlying video sequences (`bdd_b24d283f`, `bdd_b255cd6c`, `dawn_snow`, `dawn_fog`), violating asymptotic cluster assumptions.
3. **Single-Channel Acoustic Data:** Monophonic audio provides no Direction of Arrival (DOA) or spatial coordinates.
4. **Speaker Diversity Deficit:** Test set distress cries represent only one novel human speaker; $45.1\%$ of dataset consists of synthetic TTS.
5. **Propeller Acoustic Masking:** While ambient noise augmentations were tested, real-world UAV boundary-layer aeroacoustic noise from high-RPM brushless motors requires field acoustic isolation.
